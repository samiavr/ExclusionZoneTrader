title=[B42] Exclusion Zone Trader - 隔離地域外無線トレーダー
description=[h1]Exclusion Zone Trader (Build 42)[/h1]
description=[i]"The Knox quarantine line held. Outside, civilization goes on—while we were left behind in the cage."[/i]
description=
description=Trade with the outside civilian and scavenger logistics network beyond the Knox Exclusion Zone via emergency radio ([b]104.8 MHz[/b])!
description=Call in helicopter supply drops with searchlights, sell zombie spoils & pre-outbreak records, and defend your Landing Zone (LZ) against incoming hordes.
description=
description=[b]■ MultiPlayer Status (Notice)[/b]
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Single Player[/b]: Fully Tested & Highly Stable.
description=[*] [b]Multiplayer[/b]: Experimental. Built natively on Client/Server commands and container sync, but extensive multi-client stress testing is still underway. Bug reports and feedback are warmly welcomed!
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Key Features[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Radio Trading (104.8 MHz)[/b]: Find a Scavenger Flyer on zombies or tune any HAM radio / walkie-talkie directly to [b]104.8 MHz[/b].
description=[*] [b]Helicopter Drops & Stealth Drones[/b]: Helicopters fly to your outdoor LZ with searchlights, drawing huge zombie hordes! Or pay +200 CR for a silent Stealth Drone delivery.
description=[*] [b]Smart Arms & Ammo Duffel Bags[/b]: Guaranteed matched magazine (1x) + loose ammo (5-20 rounds) whenever a firearm is rolled, plus extra supply slots (Max 1 gun per bag).
description=[*] [b]Bulk Ammo Order Bonus[/b]: Order 2+ boxes of the same ammo from the [b]Ammo Depot[/b] tab and get a matching magazine (or extra shells) free!
description=[*] [b]Advanced Selling QoL[/b]: Appraises cash, jewelry, records, electronics, firearms, loaded magazines (auto-counts ammo inside!), loose ammo lots, melee weapons by condition, handcrafted furniture, and bags.
description=[*] [b]Cargo Weight Milestone Bonuses[/b]: Earn +60 to +280 CR bonus when selling heavy batches (25kg - 55kg).
description=[*] [b]Contract Farming & Specialty Bags[/b]: Deposit seeds to order fresh crops (grants Farming XP!), or order DIY Recipe Bags (all 86 skill manuals), Produce/Butcher Coolers, Mystery Furniture, and Trash Bags.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Quick Start (How to Play)[/h2]
description=[olist]
description=[*] [b]Register LZ[/b]: Right-click an outdoor container and select [b]"Designate as Trade Drop Box / LZ"[/b].
description=[*] [b]Put Items to Sell[/b]: Place cash, valuables, spare weapons, or salvage into the Drop Box.
description=[*] [b]Connect via Radio[/b]: Turn ON a radio/walkie-talkie, tune to [b]104.8 MHz[/b], and right-click it to open the Trader UI.
description=[*] [b]Signal for Drop[/b]: When notified that the transport has arrived overhead, stand by your LZ container and right-click to [b]"Request Supply Drop"[/b]!
description=[/olist]

description=[b]■ 主な機能と特徴[/b]
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]無線通信トレード (104.8 MHz)[/b]: ゾンビから拾える「スカベンジャー募集チラシ」を読むか、無線機の周波数を直接 104.8 MHz に合わせて取引画面を展開。
description=[*] [b]輸送ヘリ空輸 ＆ 無音ステルスドローン[/b]: ヘリ投下時は夜間サーチライト演出と爆音で周囲のゾンビが大集結！安全重視なら追加料金（+200 CR）で無音ドローン配送も選択可能。
description=[*] [b]銃器ダッフルバッグのセット保証[/b]: 銃本体が当選すると「対応マガジン1個＋対応バラ弾5〜20発」が確定同梱され、残りの枠にも弾薬等が充填（1バッグ銃最大1挺）。
description=[*] [b]弾薬庫（Ammo）＆ まとめ買いボーナス[/b]: 日替わり在庫の弾薬箱を指名買い可能。同じ弾薬箱を2箱以上買うと対応マガジン（またはバラ弾）が1個無料でおまけ同梱！
description=[*] [b]スマート売却査定＆積載ボーナス[/b]: 現金・貴金属・資料・電子廃材に加え、銃器、装填済みマガジン（弾抜き不要で内部弾薬も合算査定）、バラ弾、近接武器（美品/使用感あり/消耗品）、自作家具、カバン類を買取。25kg〜55kgの大量納品で最大+280 CRの積載ボーナス！
description=[*] [b]契約農園 ＆ 特化バッグ[/b]: 種を預けて新鮮な作物を交換（耕作XP獲得）、DIY技術・クラフトレシピ雑誌特化バッグ（全86種厳選）、生鮮・精肉バッグ、ミステリー家具配送など。
description=[/list]
description=
description=[b]■ 遊び方（クイックスタート）[/b]
description=[olist]
description=[*] [b]LZ登録[/b]: 屋外のコンテナを右クリックし `[交易ドロップボックス/LZに指定]` を選択。
description=[*] [b]不用品投入[/b]: ドロップボックスに売却したい物資や現金を入れる。
description=[*] [b]無線発注[/b]: 無線機の電源を入れて `104.8 MHz` に合わせ、右クリック `[無線取引ネットワークに接続]` から発注（下取り相殺も可能）。
description=[*] [b]投下合図[/b]: 上空到着の無線連絡が来たら、LZコンテナの隣で右クリック `[支援物資の投下を要請]` で合図を送って物資を回収！
